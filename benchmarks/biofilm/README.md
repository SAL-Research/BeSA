# Biofilm Growth

**Status:** Under development.

Agent-based simulation of erosion and sloughing during growth of
*Pseudomonas aeruginosa* biofilms, using BioDynaMo.

Reference: Bournes et al., "Agent-based modeling of erosion and sloughing
during growth of Pseudomonas aeruginosa biofilms," *npj Biofilms and
Microbiomes*, June 2026.
